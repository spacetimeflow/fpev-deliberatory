# Frame: the charge, the standard, the template, the form

Evidence marks: `[owner]` the evaluator's words or ruling · `[run]` observed in recorded work · `[external]` an outside authority · `[inferred]` reasoned, with what would falsify it.

## The charge

- Read the request and the institution's guidelines first. Note the standard, the review period, what the letter must address, and what it must not. [run]
- Evaluate against the host institution's standard, not the evaluator's own institution or record. [run]
- Where two periods or two reviews are combined, confirm how to treat them with the staff member responsible rather than guessing. [run]
- Put open questions to the right person, as one short list. [run]

## Template and form

- Start from the evaluator's previous letter of the same kind, "with minimal but necessary updates". [owner]
- Find out the form the institution requires. A required form, not a document, may be the deliverable. [run]
- The evaluator decides the date, letterhead and signature. [owner]

## Other people

- Name who else contributes and when. Wait for their input before drafting their part. [owner]
- Do not work in a folder shared with colleagues. Work locally and copy results there only when asked. [owner]

## Plan

- List what waits for whom, with dates, and work only on what does not wait. [owner]
- Settle early whether one combined letter or separate letters are required. [run]

## Tripwires

- The standard is not known: stop and ask the evaluator. [run]
- A step needs someone else's input that has not arrived: wait, and say so in the handoff. [owner]
