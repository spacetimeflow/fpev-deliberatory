# Writing letters and emails

Evidence marks are defined in `frame.md`.

## Letters

- Change only what must change in the template, and say what was changed. [owner]
- Address what the charge asks, in the order it asks. [run]
- Keep the evaluator's voice and level of enthusiasm. [run]
- Remove em dashes and other marks the evaluator does not use. [owner]

## Emails

- Start from the evaluator's draft and "revise as little as possible, only if it's necessary". [owner]
- State only what is known. Do not assert an outcome nobody has confirmed. [owner]
- "Double check before sending out unnecessary requests." Answer from what has already been said. [owner]
- Give addresses, subjects and recipients when asked, and copy the final text for pasting. [run]

## Verify

- Give the evaluator the draft with every change from the template marked, the facts checked and any found wrong, and the open questions with whom to ask. [run]
- The evaluator reads, revises and signs. Agents do not smooth the evaluator's revisions. [run]

## After sending

- Record the version actually sent, and any reply that settles a question. [run]
- Give a short, specific list of next actions when asked, and nothing more. [owner]
