# Evidence: facts and judgments

Evidence marks are defined in `frame.md`.

## Facts about the candidate

- Check every fact the letter states (titles, dates, venues, counts, awards, grants) against the candidate's own materials, and public sources where needed. [run]
- Report inconsistencies inside the materials, such as dates that disagree between documents, as a short list for the evaluator. [run]
- Prefer the candidate's own terms for their work. [run]
- Re-check figures that change, such as citation counts, on the day of signing. [run]

## The evaluator's judgments

- Record the evaluator's judgments as given, then check them against the materials and point out any disagreement. [owner]
- Do not add praise, ranking or criticism the evaluator did not give. [run]

## Several evaluators

- When consolidating colleagues' views, keep each view attributed until the evaluator merges them. [run]
- Collect inconsistencies among evaluators in one document, with the question each raises and who should answer it. [owner]

## Confidentiality

- Candidate materials, scores and contacts stay in the case's private folder and leave only in the letter the evaluator sends. [run]
- Other evaluators' drafts are confidential to the committee. [inferred] Falsified if the institution's procedure shares them with the candidate.
