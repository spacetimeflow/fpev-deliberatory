# Literature and novelty

Evidence marks are defined in `frame-a-version.md`.

- For each result, relate it to known results, with citations. [owner]
- Read the closest prior work before claiming novelty, and record each result's status after reading: new, refined, or known. [run]
- Narrow overclaiming. State the contribution precisely and credit every borrowed piece. [run]
- Use the researcher's literature collection first, and add papers they download. [owner]
- Check references and citations for accuracy before submission. [owner]
