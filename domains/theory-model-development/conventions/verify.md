# Verify: the researcher's reading

Evidence marks are defined in `frame-a-version.md`.

- Open each Verify phase with the researcher's directive, recorded verbatim in its handoff. [run]
- Give the researcher one page per version: what changed, each result's status (proved, conjecture, known), the numbers recomputed, and the contradictions found. [run]
- Expect a long series of phases, one per directive. [run]
- When a directive changes the frame, record it, and decide with the researcher whether it starts a new version. [owner]
- Mechanical checks belong to Execute; Verify is the researcher's reading and judgment. [owner]
