# Exploration pieces

A domain shape for the exploration piece: a written brainstorm that develops the owner's ideas against a source and records what was read. Read this first. The `conventions/` say how.

## Purpose

To think in writing with an agent while keeping the thinking the owner's and the evidence honest. The owner asks for a piece ("Write an exploration piece …"), asks for it to "follow the formats of the recent ones", and keeps extending it ("Update the exploration pieces with these new discussions/ideas"). A piece that proves its worth can be elevated into a project.

## Scope

One piece, from its first prompt through its follow-ups. A piece decides nothing, and turning one into an FPEV project is a separate step the owner takes.

## The situation

An agent can write a long, confident essay from a summary in minutes. In a personal research corpus this risk compounds. Agent framings get cited later as the owner's, summaries pass for readings, and claims about outside work go unchecked. Follow-up prompts can silently rewrite what the piece first said, and a piece reported as updated may not be.

## Deliverable

A markdown piece in the owner's explorations folder, in the house format: a date-titled file, front matter that records the owner's prompts verbatim and what was read, an answer in brief, numbered sections, honest limits, and a short list for the owner. Follow-ups are appended as new sections.

## Stages and roles

A piece is a light cycle. The owner's prompt frames it, the agent researches and writes, and the owner's reading verifies it, often by a follow-up.

- **The owner** supplies the idea, the source and the notes, and judges the piece.
- **Agents** transcribe notes, read sources, search, write and record.


| Stage | Criterion | The domain supplies | Stays with the person |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frame | understandability | the owner's prompt, notes and named pieces | the idea and the question |
| Plan | conformance | the house format; what to read | little; a piece is light |
| Execute | quality | reading, searching with links, writing with voices marked | nothing routine |
| Verify | insight | the answer in brief; the short list to decide; the evidence note | judging the piece; follow-ups; elevation to a project |
## Cadence

One prompt, one piece, then follow-ups as the owner thinks. Each follow-up is its own short cycle.

## Record

The piece's front matter carries its record: the prompts, the related files, and the evidence note.

## Kernel

These hold in every piece:

- **The owner's ideas are the frame,** and their prompts are recorded verbatim.
- **State what was read,** in full, in part, searched, or not read.
- **Mark who says what:** the owner, the sources, and the agent's own readings.
- **Link outside claims,** and say when they rest on summaries.
- **Follow the house format.**
- **Append follow-ups;** do not rewrite silently.
- **Check the file before reporting it updated.**
- **A piece decides nothing.**

## Envelopes

Agents may act alone inside these bounds. Outside them, the owner decides.

- Transcribe the owner's notes and images in the folder.
- Read sources, search the web, and write or extend the piece.

Agents may never present their own framing as the owner's, or turn a piece into a project unasked.

## What to internalize, and what stays on file

**Internalize:** whose idea each paragraph carries, and what was actually read.

**Keep on file:** the format template and the record of earlier pieces.

## Untested

- Whether the format helps later work cite pieces correctly. No audit has been done.
