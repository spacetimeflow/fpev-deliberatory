# Extending a piece

Evidence marks are defined in `write.md`.

- Record each follow-up prompt in the front matter (prompted-by-2, -3, …) and append its discussion as a new section. [run]
- Revise earlier text only when the owner asks, and note the revision. [run]
- After writing, check the file on disk and confirm the update before saying it is done. [owner]
- The owner's reading is Verify, and their follow-up prompts are its record. Keep the answer in brief and the list to decide current as sections are appended. [run]
- If the owner elevates a piece to a project, start an FPEV cycle with the piece and its references as core context. [owner]
