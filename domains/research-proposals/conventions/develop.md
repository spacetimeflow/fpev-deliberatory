# Developing a proposal

Evidence marks: `[owner]` the investigator's words or ruling · `[run]` observed in recorded work · `[external]` an outside authority · `[inferred]` reasoned, with what would falsify it.

## Frame

- Read the solicitation and the sponsor's current proposal guide, and record its required sections, page and word limits, and summary headings, with the date. [external]
- State the thesis in one sentence. [run]
- Name who writes which part. Co-investigators own theirs. [run]
- On a collaborative proposal, extract what concerns the investigator's own part, and suggest revisions to it rather than rewriting others' parts. [owner]

## Restructure, do not paste

- Recast a paper into the sponsor's structure. An article's sections are not a project description. [run]
- Put prior results where the sponsor wants them, in the sponsor's terms. [run]

## Claim status

- Mark every property and result as established, preliminary or proposed. Unproven properties "should all be presented as proposed work". [owner]

## Execute: checks before Verify

- Check the thesis appears coherently in every section. [run]
- Compile, check page counts, undefined references and citations, and fix overfull lines. [run]
- Map each of the investigator's comments from the last round to the change made. [run]

## Verify

- Give the investigator one view: the claim-status table, the storyline check, the limits report, and the comment-to-action map. [run]
- The investigator comments and decides. Each new round of comments is mapped in Execute before the next Verify. [run]
