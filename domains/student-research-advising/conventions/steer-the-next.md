# Steering the next version

Evidence marks are defined in `verify-a-version.md`.

- Turn the advisor's meeting notes and handwritten ideas into the next version's references before framing. [run]
- Ask whether the account can be simplified, and propose the simpler one. [owner]
- When the advisor's verification grows the theory, start a new version in the advisor's space and credit both lines of work. [run]
- A major change gets a new version number, with the earlier version kept and its challenges noted. [run]
- Define the next version's deliverables before work starts, so its result can be judged. [owner]
