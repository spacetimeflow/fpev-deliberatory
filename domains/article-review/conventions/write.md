# Writing the report

Evidence marks are defined in `frame.md`.

## Whose comments

- Organize the major comments around the reviewer's own questions. Every major comment traces to one of them. [run]
- An agent's finding enters the report only when the reviewer adopts it. The reviewer once withdrew a comment the agent had built, with the words "not mine". [run]
- Keep the reviewer's tone and verdict. Do not add praise, hedges or demands the reviewer did not make. [run]
- Never drop something the reviewer said. Carry it into a comment, or record why it was set aside. [run]

## Few comments that matter

- Pick the few comments "where the authors can meaningfully improve the mansucript". [owner]
- Cut minor comments the reviewer is not willing to verify. In one cycle all of them were cut. [run]
- Follow the reviewer's length rule when given, such as three sentences per major comment, or three paragraphs in all. [owner]
- A comment states the claim, the evidence, and what the authors should do. [run]

## The recommendation

- Leave a placeholder until the body is verified. The reviewer writes or confirms the recommendation at Verify. [run]
- Base the recommendation on the contribution, in the reviewer's words. [run]

## Ready to paste

- Ask, or follow the venue, for the submission format: separate comments to the authors and to the editor. [run]
- Remove rendered math that breaks when pasted; write symbols as plain text. [run]
- Apply the reviewer's style rules, such as no em dashes and plain, direct sentences. [run]
- Check length limits and character sets before handing over. [run]

## Verify

- Give the reviewer, in one place, the draft, the spot-check list of recalled references, and the spots to double-check: "Flag areas that I particularly need to double check". [owner]
- Expect several rounds. Record each reshaping, with the reviewer's words, in its own phase. [run]
- Move what the reviewer calls not theirs into private notes. [run]

## After submission

- Record the text actually submitted, verbatim, as the final version, and keep the earlier drafts. [run]
- If the submitted text differs from the agent's last draft, the submitted text governs. Note any factual discrepancy for the reviewer without changing it. [run]
