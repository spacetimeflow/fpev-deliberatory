# Checking: the ledger

Evidence marks are defined in `frame.md`.

## The claim ledger

- Keep one row per claim the report will make: the claim, where in the paper, the evidence, the status (verified, recalled, open), and which of the reviewer's questions it serves. [run]
- Start the ledger before the report. Every number, quotation and equation the report cites must have a row. [run]
- A report with an open row does not go to Verify. [run]

## Against the paper

- Verify each quotation and page by searching the extracted text. One quoted phrase turned out to come from a different section than claimed. [run]
- Recompute every number the report cites, keeping the scripts in the review's folder. [run]
- Check equations, definitions and proofs the report relies on. "Make sure the equations are correct." [owner]
- When the report says the paper does not mention something, show the search that found zero occurrences. [run]
- Check that an assumption the report attributes to the paper is actually in the paper. One comment rested on an assumption the paper never made, and the reviewer had it dropped. [run]

## Independence

- The agent that drafted a comment does not certify it. A second pass, by another agent or the reviewer, checks it. [run]
- When the reviewer asks whether their own recommendation is well founded, check the paper against it and report disagreements plainly. [owner]

## Honest status

- Report a step that could not be done as not done, with the reason and what would unblock it. [run]
