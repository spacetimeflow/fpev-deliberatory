# Understanding: the gap wiki and the literature

Evidence marks are defined in `frame.md`.

## The gap wiki

- Write one short entry per concept, model or method the review depends on, at the level set in the Frame. [run]
- An entry is a draft until the reviewer marks it learned. Agents never mark it. [run]
- Relate each entry to where the manuscript uses it, by section, without copying its text. [run]
- Keep the wiki inside the review's folder, since it is shaped by a confidential paper. [inferred] Falsified if entries are routinely generic enough to share.

## Key references and novelty

- Find the key references by the paper's topic and its own citations, and use them to judge novelty. [owner]
- Judge novelty against what the references show, not against the paper's own framing of its contribution. [owner]
- Prefer an inventory to a judgment: where something is missing or repeated, count it and give the locations. [run]
- Read the reviewer's own related work when the reviewer points to it. If access is refused, say so rather than working around it. [run]
- Search with topic words and author-year citations. Do not paste manuscript text into a search. [inferred] Falsified if a venue permits it.

## When the network is unavailable

- Mark each literature claim: **[V]** verified this session (the manuscript, the reviewer's papers, a derivation) or **[K]** recalled from the model's knowledge. [run]
- A [K] item never appears in the report as a fact about the paper. It goes on a short list the reviewer spot-checks before submitting. [run]

## Competence

- Note which questions lie outside the reviewer's field. The report can leave them to another reviewer and say so to the editor. [run]
