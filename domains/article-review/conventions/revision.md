# Revision rounds

Evidence marks are defined in `frame.md`.

## The response map

- List each of the reviewer's earlier comments with four entries: what was asked, what the authors say they did, where the change appears, and the reviewer's assessment (addressed, partly, not addressed). [run]
- Verify each claimed change in the revised manuscript itself. A response letter can overstate, including about length. [run]
- Read the other reviewers' comments and the replies to them, for overlap and for new problems. [run]

## No new comments

- Follow up the earlier comments. Do not add new ones unless the reviewer asks. "I don't want to add new ones." [owner]
- New material introduced by the revision may be checked, for example terms used without definition. Raise it only when the reviewer agrees. [run]
- Compare terms across versions when the revision introduces vocabulary. Count each term's occurrences in the old and new texts. [run]

## The report

- Write in the reviewer's voice, acknowledging what improved. [run]
- Keep the follow-ups few and say whether the recommendation changes. [run]
- Add to the original review file when the reviewer keeps one, rather than starting a new one. [owner]
